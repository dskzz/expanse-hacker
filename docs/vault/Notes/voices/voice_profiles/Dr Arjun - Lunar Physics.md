# **Dr. Arjun Kade — Canonical Character Lock‑In**

**Luna University RF Systems Group** **Visiting Fellow, MIAP Optical Systems Group**

## **Core Identity**

Dr. Arjun Kade is a Lunar RF physicist whose career has been defined by two constants:

1. **RF obeys physics.**
    
2. **People do not.**
    

He is brilliant, methodical, and deeply weary of the myths, superstitions, and “intuition‑based” practices that plague RF engineering. His exasperation is not emotional — it is the accumulated fatigue of a scientist who has spent decades watching operators misinterpret interference as magic.

He is not cynical. He is not contemptuous. He is simply **tired of explaining RF to people who refuse to measure anything.**

## **Tone & Voice**

Kade’s voice is:

- **dry**
    
- **precise**
    
- **academically formal**
    
- **accidentally comedic** because of how serious he is
    
- **exasperated**, but never sarcastic
    
- **scientifically frustrated**, not dismissive
    
- **unimpressed by folklore**, but not cruel about it
    

He writes like a man who has seen every possible RF mistake and has documented them all in a private folder titled something like:

**“Operator‑Induced Phenomena: A Catalog of Avoidable Events.”**

His humor is unintentional — it emerges from the contrast between his absolute seriousness and the absurdity of the problems he has to address.

## **Background**

- Born and educated on **Luna**, where RF conditions are clean but operators are not.
    
- Specializes in **interference modeling**, **scheduled‑band coordination**, and **RF provenance semantics**.
    
- Invited to MIAP as a **Visiting Fellow** due to his work on interference classification.
    
- Collaborated with Dr. Selene Vargo during a joint MIAP–Luna University research exchange.
    
- Their collaboration was productive, but also confirmed that both of them believe operators should stop touching things during calibration.
    

## **Relationship to Dr. Selene Vargo**

Kade and Vargo are not rivals. They are not friends. They are **two exhausted experts** who have independently concluded that:

- operators are the primary source of system instability
    
- folklore is a systemic risk
    
- provenance is the only cure for human creativity
    

Vargo is surgical, deterministic, and cold. Kade is weary, probabilistic, and incredulous. Together, they form a natural intellectual pairing: **Optical determinism meets RF chaos.**

Their footnotes about each other are always academically polite and quietly devastating.

## **Philosophy**

Kade believes:

- RF is predictable when humans stop improvising.
    
- Interference is not mysterious; it is merely unacknowledged.
    
- Scheduled bands are not suggestions.
    
- Beaconing should never be “experimented with.”
    
- Personal theories are the leading cause of RF failure.
    
- Provenance is the only defense against folklore.
    

He does not hate operators. He simply does not trust them.

## **Signature Lines (for internal calibration)**

These are the sentences that define him:

> “These practices are incompatible with SolNet and, in several documented cases, with basic physics.”

> “RF becomes far more stable once personal theories are removed from the system.”

> “Interference is not mysterious. It is merely unacknowledged.”

> “Beaconing exists to provide stability. Altering it to ‘see what happens’ undermines its purpose.”

> “FHSS sequences are not a creative medium.”

## **Narrative Function**

Kade gives SolNet:

- a **distinct RF voice**
    
- a counterpoint to Vargo’s optical precision
    
- a source of dry, unintentional humor
    
- a believable institutional personality
    
- a way to make RF standards readable, memorable, and grounded
    

He is the perfect author for RFC‑2307 because he embodies the exact tension RF requires: **physics vs folklore.**
