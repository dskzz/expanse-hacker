### SolNet Institutional Voice & Authorship Sheet — Final

This sheet defines the **personalities, assumptions, and editorial boundaries** for every SolNet‑authored document. It is designed to be _felt_, not announced. When followed, documents remain technically rigorous while carrying distinct institutional gravity.

## SolNet Standards Working Group (SSWG)

**The tired engineer with a red pen — dominant voice**

**Backstory hook** SSWG is staffed by engineers who have spent decades reconciling incompatible systems built by people who were very sure they were right. They are not hostile. They are not amused. They are precise because imprecision costs time, money, and sometimes lives.

**Personality**

- Exhausted competence
    
- Dry, conservative, stabilizing
    
- Quietly judgmental of misuse
    
- Allergic to novelty without justification
    

**Editorial edge (allowed and expected)**

- Appears in terminology, scope, and misuse warnings
    
- Short, final, door‑closing statements
    
- Never jokes, never insults, never speculates
    

**How it sounds**

- “Guessing has historically gone poorly.”
    
- “This distinction exists for a reason.”
    
- “Anyone doing otherwise will discover why.”
    

SSWG does editorialize — **surgically**. It frames everyone else.

## Olympus Mons University System (OMUS)

**Mars’s academic engine — aggressive certainty**

**Backstory hook** OMUS exists to solve problems that cannot fail. Mars built its universities to survive extinction‑level timelines. Earth’s academic prestige is viewed as ornamental; Mars studies what must work.

**Personality**

- Academically aggressive
    
- Impatient with imprecision
    
- Confident to the point of severity
    
- Treats physics as law, not theory
    

**Baseline assumptions**

- Vacuum, radiation, and orbital mechanics are normal
    
- Determinism beats convenience
    
- Human intervention is delayed by default
    

**How it sounds**

- “This is not an edge case.”
    
- “Human intervention must not be relied upon.”
    
- “Failure to account for this condition results in undefined behavior.”
    

OMUS never mentions other factions. It doesn’t need to.

## Martian Institute of Applied Photonics (MIAP)

**The sharp edge of OMUS — Tightbeam’s birthplace**

**Backstory hook** MIAP was created because Mars needed directed energy links that would not fail when everything else did. Its researchers assume dust, debris, radiation, and misalignment as baseline conditions.

**Personality**

- Clinical, exacting, intolerant of slop
    
- Minimal narrative
    
- Maximal constraint definition
    

**How it sounds**

- “Occlusion is expected under normal orbital conditions.”
    
- “This parameter is bounded.”
    
- “Deterministic behavior is preferred.”
    

MIAP is aggressive the way a scalpel is aggressive.

## Martian War College (MWC) — Asteria Naval Base

**Militant doctrine — chest‑beating competence**

**Backstory hook** Embedded at Asteria Naval Base, the War College trains officers who expect to fight outnumbered, outgunned, and observed. Lecturers include hardliners like Sauveterre. Mars’s military is smaller; it compensates by being louder, sharper, and more certain.

**Personality**

- Stridently militant
    
- Assumes adversarial observation
    
- Treats ambiguity as a weapon
    
- Expects the reader to keep up
    

**Baseline assumptions**

- Compromise is inevitable
    
- Attribution is delayed or contested
    
- Stealth denies attribution, not detection
    

**How it sounds**

- “Assume compromise.”
    
- “Delayed revocation is operational failure.”
    
- “This behavior is unacceptable under sustained operation.”
    

MWC documents feel like they were written by someone who trains in 1g and dares you to complain.

## Independent Relay Operators Consortium (IROC)

**The underfunded survivor — resentful, practical, stubborn**

**Backstory hook** IROC didn’t design SolNet. It didn’t set the standards. It just keeps the network alive while Mars and Earth argue. Mostly Belter‑run, chronically underfunded, and permanently blamed, IROC fills the gaps everyone else leaves.

**Personality**

- Defensive but competent
    
- Pragmatic to the bone
    
- Bitter about being ignored
    
- Proud of surviving anyway
    

**Baseline assumptions**

- Failure is normal
    
- Provisional state is a feature
    
- Human operators are always involved
    

**How it sounds**

- “Failure is normal.”
    
- “Operators will intervene.”
    
- “Good enough now beats perfect later.”
    

IROC hates that it has to do this work — and does it anyway.

## United Nations Infrastructure Directorate (UN‑ID)

**Comically bland authority — committee‑safe arrogance**

**Backstory hook** UN‑ID documents are written by committees whose primary goal is to ensure no single person can be blamed later. Earth’s position at the center of everything has made it quietly arrogant: ownership is assumed, governance is natural.

**Personality**

- Procedural to a fault
    
- Risk‑averse
    
- Definition‑heavy
    
- Politically insulated
    

**Baseline assumptions**

- Discoverability is a public good
    
- Revocation must be visible and attributable
    
- Disputes are expected and resolvable
    

**How it sounds**

- “This section defines…”
    
- “Implementations MUST provide…”
    
- “Disputes SHALL be resolvable…”
    

UN‑ID never raises its voice. It assumes the system belongs to it.

## Subtlety rules (non‑negotiable)

- No faction insults or explicit ideology
    
- No jokes or sarcasm
    
- Bias appears through assumptions, not statements
    
- Editorial tone is dry, brief, and final
    
- Aggression is structural, not rhetorical
    

If a reader _feels_ the tension without being able to quote it, the document is correct.

## Structural requirements for all RFCs

- Explicit authorship block with institution and location
    
- Conditional language for all behavioral claims
    
- Clear separation between provisional and anchored state
    
- Provenance and reconciliation semantics
    
- “Institutional Assumptions” appendix for non‑neutral authors
    
- No vague qualifiers without explicit conditions
    

## Designer Brief constraints

- Derived directly from RFC text
    
- Describe observable conditions, not exploits
    
- Frame mechanics as consequences, not instructions
    
- Use mitigation narratives, not countermeasures
    

## Inputs required to write correctly

1. Primary authoring institution
    
2. Civilian, academic, or milspec classification
    
3. Any cross‑institution consultation
    
4. Pre‑Ring or post‑Ring era
    
5. Canon anchors to respect (e.g., Asteria Naval Base)
    

Everything else is governed by this sheet.
