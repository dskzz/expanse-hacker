### Protocol substrate doctrine

- **Frozen core:** L0/L1 base stays minimal and stable.
    
- **Extension surface:** all new behavior ships as **versioned profiles/modules** using TLV containers.
    
- **Interop rule:** unknown TLVs are safely skippable; no mandatory L1 expansion for features.
    

### Namespaces and politics doctrine

- **Abundant namespaces:** TLV space is plentiful; innovation is cheap.
    
- **Deterministic decoding:** identity of a TLV is **(namespace, key)**.
    
- **Political behavior lives in policy:** trust domains decide what they honor/forward/charge for—not the parser.
    

### Evidence and anchoring doctrine

- **Receipts are common; anchoring is conditional.**
    
- **Anchoring is a gradient:** core systems anchor more; fringe anchors less.
    
- **Provisional-first:** partition tolerance is normal; reconciliation is a first-class workflow.
    

### Ordered–chaotic gradient doctrine

- **Inner system:** more governance, more certification, more enforcement.
    
- **Outer system:** more improvisation, more local norms, more “good enough.”
    
- **Specs must support both without contradiction**—profiles express the difference.
    

### Trust doctrine

- **Trust is explicit:** every “important” claim has provenance (pointer, signature, policy reference).
    
- **Trust is local-first:** domains can refuse, degrade, or surcharge foreign semantics.
    
- **Trust is reconstructable:** disputes can be replayed from receipts + pointers.
    

### Simulation and LoD doctrine

- **Assume it works at distance:** abstract behavior when zoomed out.
    
- **Expose the machinery when zoomed in:** costs, delays, friction, and failures become gameplay.

## Standards Gravity Gradient

> **SolNet operates under a Standards Gravity Gradient.** **Adherence to standards is strongest near centers of physical and institutional gravity and weakens with distance.** **Specifications must function correctly under partial, degraded, or absent adherence.** **No layer may assume universal compliance.**


**Nodes may infer a local Conformance Score based on observed protocol behavior.** **Conformance reflects adherence to standards, not trust or authority.** **Conformance expectations increase with standards gravity.** **Systems must function correctly even when conformance is low or absent.**
