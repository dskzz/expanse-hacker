## The Solwide Heiress (working title)

**Status:** concept only, names not yet invented. Do not build with any of
the placeholder names below — "Solwide Corporation," "the broker," "the
client" are stand-ins, not final.

**Origin:** a deliberately genericized version of a very specific
real-world story beat (an heiress discovering her father's company's
secret human experimentation program), reworked to drop every borrowed
name while keeping the shape of the premise, which isn't ownable by
anyone. See the session's IP-cleanup discussion for the full reasoning;
short version below.

**Setup:** The player is hired by a data broker (a fixer/handler role —
think someone who trades in information and favors, not a programmer)
acting on behalf of a client: a wealthy, fairly skilled scion of a
sol-wide megacorporation. She has low-level insider network access
nobody takes seriously — she's family, not a threat, and she's clever
enough to have leveraged that casual trust into basic espionage. She
started out looking for something mundane: evidence her father is
embezzling, or otherwise dirty in some ordinary corporate-crime way.
What she actually finds is far worse — indications of illegal human
experimentation with some kind of bioweapon, at a remote, classified
research outpost on one of Saturn's moons.

**Why Phoebe, specifically:** Phoebe is real, scientifically believed to
be a captured object — likely originating outside the solar system
entirely, on an irregular retrograde orbit inconsistent with forming in
place around Saturn. "The one body in the system that didn't originate
here is the right place to hide something that doesn't belong here
either" is a real-science-grounded creative choice, not something
anyone owns. Safe to use Phoebe itself; just don't reproduce the specific
bioweapon/plot details tied to any existing franchise's version of this
idea.

**The player's job:** build a relay, routed through the client's own
yacht (which has some legitimate presence on the corporate network), into
the remote research outpost, and extract evidence of the experiments.

**Key design beat — the yacht is a false positive, not the answer:**
Routing through her yacht should get the player something real but
incomplete — confirmation that *something* is happening at the outpost,
not what. The corporation is competent enough that her ship's access
doesn't reach deep enough to close the case on its own. The actual way
in should be something mundane and human rather than another technical
exploit — e.g., she's been quietly reading her father's own mail server,
and that's the real thread to pull. This mirrors the corpus's own
running theme (a clean signal tells you something happened, not what —
Briscoe's QBER doctrine, the DRE's "witness, not notary" rule, Trellis's
paperwork-vs-reality gap) and makes for a better two-beat mission
structure than a single clean solve: beat one (yacht) raises the stakes
without resolving anything; beat two (mail server) is the real,
more human intrusion puzzle.

**Still needs inventing before this gets built:**
- An original name for the parent "Solwide Corporation."
- An original subsidiary/shell-company brand for the outpost's own
  computer systems (distinct from the parent, which is itself realistic
  — corporate structuring as its own worldbuilding beat).
- An original identity for the client's yacht. Not shown on-screen in
  detail (schematic only, per plan) — but its *functional profile*
  still needs to be distinct, not just renamed. Steer away from "fast
  personal racer registered to a famous family" as a silhouette, since
  that profile alone is recognizable regardless of what it's called.
  Candidate direction discussed: an unglamorous, deliberately
  boring-looking vessel she either bought used and off-books or
  quietly retrofitted herself — gives her a reason to have it that
  isn't "status symbol," and breaks the recognizable shape rather than
  just relabeling it.
- A name for the client herself (not a disguised version of any real
  name — a genuinely new character).
- A specific nature for the bioweapon/experiments, distinct from any
  existing franchise's specific depicted substance.

**Explicitly avoid:** anything resembling Mao-Kwikowski as a visible
in-network brand name, the word "protomolecule" or any closely
equivalent coined term, any ship name recognizable as a specific
existing franchise's vessel, and any direct restaging of a specific
named character's discovery arc beat-for-beat.
