# Flashpoints

Status: tracking doc, created 2026-11-24. Where `people-pass-queue.md`
tracks every unnamed figure awaiting a name, this file tracks every
live, unresolved tension across the corpus loaded enough to actually
erupt into something — not just an undecided detail, a standing fault
line. Add to this list as new ones come up or get identified; an
entry graduates off this list only when it's actually resolved in its
home file, not when it's merely been written about.

## The Jovian sovereignty crisis

Home file: `callisto.md` (with `europa.md` and `peale-extraction.md`
as supporting context). The Callisto Home Rule League's push for
self-governance, its maximalist United Moons splinter's pan-Jovian
independence ambition, and the UN/MDR/OSL's own negotiated Concord
Settlement (a real partition, not an invented complication) are all
live at once, with three mutually exclusive endings on the table and
nothing here deciding which wins. The single largest unresolved
political thread in the corpus right now.

## The Massacre at Thisbe Station's unfinished business

Home file: `thisbe-station.md`. The founding trauma itself is settled
history, but its consequences aren't: the live helium-3 crisis
(regional scarcity colliding with the cooperative's own survival) and
the open question of who currently leads the cooperative and what
they actually decide to do with the discovery remain unresolved,
decades after the massacre itself.

## Lunar resource depletion

Home file: none yet — newly identified here, not yet given a
dedicated writeup. Luna's own helium-3 deposits (`armstrong-city.md`)
are real, but genuinely finite: solar-wind-implanted He-3 in regolith
sits at real parts-per-billion concentrations, and decades of
sustained industrial extraction is exactly the process that works
through the easiest, richest deposits first — the same real pattern
as actual terrestrial mining history. A believable, well-earned
consequence: Luna's easy reserves are plausibly running thin by now,
pushing extraction toward harder, lower-grade regolith at rising
cost. That doesn't just coincide with several threads already built
elsewhere — it helps *explain* them: MDR's own push for Phobos/Deimos
fuel independence (`mars-political-structure.md`), Frostline
Harvesting's cartel-style defensiveness over its regional supply
(`europa.md`), and the real strategic stakes behind the fight over
Europa's deuterium all read differently once Luna's own well is
visibly running dry rather than just being prudently diversified away
from. Worth a dedicated pass of its own; flagged here first so the
connection isn't lost before that happens.
