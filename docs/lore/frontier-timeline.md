# The Frontier Timeline: Why Expansion Happened When and How It Did

Status: locked, 2026-11-27. Companion to `demographics.md` and
`os-lineages.md`'s own §1 timeline (which this one treats as fixed —
the Seven Day War through the Anti-Solar Accord and the Frontier
Opportunity Program are not re-derived here, only built around). Where
`demographics.md` establishes *which* cultural threads are load-
bearing and why, this file establishes *when* each wave of settlement
actually happened and what specific, real-world-shaped condition on
Earth pushed it outward — so each wave explains why the next one
became necessary, rather than reading as a list of unrelated firsts.
All dates approximate and adjustable, same standing caveat as
`os-lineages.md`'s own.

Four phases: ambition and rivalry (nobody fleeing anything, yet);
release valve (real desperation, real deaths); scarcity (the easy
reserves already spent); and the Rupture sitting between the first two
and everything after. "Now" is ~2350, same anchor `os-lineages.md`
already fixed.

## Phase 0: the propulsion breakthrough (~2090s–2115)

Cheap ship *construction* (building interplanetary hulls from space
material instead of lifting mass out of Earth's well) doesn't explain
cheap ship *operation* — that needs an actual propulsion breakthrough,
and this corpus didn't have one on the books yet. **Spitzer Station**,
an Earth-orbital high-energy physics program named for Lyman Spitzer
(the real inventor of the stellarator, an actual mid-20th-century
fusion-confinement pioneer — same honorific convention as Lawrence,
Peale, Kivelson, Cassini, and Herschel), is where the fusion-torch
physics behind every drive in this corpus actually gets cracked.
Physics isn't engineering, though, so two corporate partners cluster
around it to build the actual hardware: **Korolev Propulsion**
(engineering the physics into a working drive — named for Sergei
Korolev, the real chief engineer behind the actual Soviet space
program) and **Vernadsky Systems** (closed-loop life support and
space-environmental engineering — named for Vladimir Vernadsky, the
real originator of the biosphere concept). Both Russian-coded
deliberately: this is the Slavic/Eastern-European thread's *first*
real seed (`demographics.md`), decades before the later wave below
adds to it — a technical elite, not a displaced population, which
makes the thread's eventual prominence earned twice over rather than
asserted once.

**Hauptmann & Voskuijlen's reach goes back to the very beginning.**
The Trust's own four members are already fixed (Okafor-Lindgren,
H&V, Meridiani Systems, Oberth Extraction, one sector each —
`corporations.md`), so Korolev and Vernadsky aren't new members —
they're H&V's own hidden-ownership pattern, playing out this early
rather than being invented fresh. Both companies needed real capital
for genuinely expensive, speculative early R&D no ordinary credit
would touch — exactly H&V's stated business, decades before the
Frontier Opportunity Program gives it a second, more famous instance.
Both technologies never stop being essential (every ship needs an
engine, every habitat needs air and water recycled), so the debt
never fully clears, and H&V ends up quietly holding real equity in
both — the same mechanism already on the books for Nova Court's
casino stake, just older and bigger. H&V's hidden hand has been on the
propulsion and life-support layer of literally everything in known
space since before the first colony ship ever burned for Mars.

A real, if quieter, instance of this corpus's habitual "everyone
actually cooperates, once, under necessity, and it doesn't last"
pattern — the same shape the OPRA moment runs on decades later, just
smaller and earlier, and just as thoroughly forgotten once the acute
need passed.

## Phase 1: ambition and rivalry (~2095–2180)

Not a golden age — Earth's own real strain is already present at the
very start, just not yet acute enough to drive mass flight. "2100 is
roughly current-state-magnified": Earth's resources already depleting,
India already facing real overpopulation and starvation pressure, the
old West already in relative decline, China already rising. Two old
rival powers racing to grab position while the ground shifts under
both of them, not expansion out of surplus confidence.

**~2095–2100 — Shackleton Base gets a real spaceport.** Luna's first
genuine foothold, basic polar ice mining running at real scale —
`armstrong-city.md`'s own "the origin, now declining" already fits
this without needing adjustment.

**~2100–2130 — small-scale asteroid mining (Mars, Moon), and a first
industrial anchor at the stable Earth-Moon L4 point.** A Western-led,
Chinese-partnered refinery/forge goes up — informally run, not yet
carrying the name it'll be known by — feeding Earth-orbital shipyards
as the industry pivots toward ships built from space material rather
than launched whole out of Earth's well. **Ceres itself gets settled
in this same window** — one of the very earliest Belt footholds, an
early Slavic technical-elite population (the same engineering culture
Korolev Propulsion and Vernadsky Systems seeded, below) getting in
early enough that VPH's own later founding reads as "generations"
afterward rather than concurrent (`ceres.md`). **Earth-orbital and lunar
hydroponic farming**, directly Harsh-Mistress-shaped, becomes real
infrastructure in this same window — grain genuinely cheap to ship
*because* a mass driver is launching it downhill into Earth's own
gravity well, not fighting out of one, feeding Earth's hungriest
populations (India chief among them) directly. The same hardware
later gets repurposed the other direction (regolith and ore up to
orbit) once the acute food crisis eases and the economy's attention
shifts to building ships instead of feeding people.

**~2130 — industrial accident, or Luddite sabotage, nearly destroys
the L4 refinery.** Real lives lost; real public confidence shaken.

**~2135–2150 — Armstrong City's actual founding.** The UN, needing to
restore public faith in space travel after the disaster, and newly
aware of just how fast Mars is rising, builds Armstrong City as a
confidence-restoring tourism and administrative project: a spaceport,
a mass driver, and — India's own direct contribution, leaning on the
same hydroponic expertise above — a ring of lunar farms. The L4
refinery is rebuilt in the same window, now running cheap enough that
Mars gets genuinely settled at scale: Mariner Valley (the Western
coalition) and Elysium (the Sino-Russian coalition) both take root
here, racing each other exactly as `mars-political-structure.md`
already establishes — now with an actual mechanism (cheap, L4-built
ships) explaining why that race became possible when it did.

**~2150–2180 — India's own exodus wave, and Vishvakarma's real
founding as a name.** India's domestic crisis (mass overpopulation,
real starvation) is acute enough now that its own orbital-farm and
lunar-farming expertise becomes the springboard for taking over and
massively expanding the L4 refinery — the same physical site running
continuously since ~2100, which is exactly why **Vishvakarma** can
still honestly be called the oldest industrial station in Sol even
though the name itself, and the culture that now runs it, both date
to India's takeover rather than the site's original founding. This is
a genuine, dangerous release valve — real lives lost — not confident
ambition, and it pumps a massive Indian diaspora out into the wider
system. China, watching its own domestic centrality recede as its
former Belt and Road partner nations rise in the vacuum, accelerates
its own push to keep pace.

## Phase 1, continued: the diasporas multiply (~2180–2250)

**~2180–2200 — ships now cheap enough that Mars itself joins the
shipbuilding race**, and other population groups launch their own
homogeneous diasporas — Pacific, broader Asian, South American
threads, none sited yet, open for later. **Jupiter's settlement
begins here**, and will go on to soak up most of the system's
settlement energy through the 2200s. **Mercury is founded late in
this same window (~2190–2200), explicitly as research, not
extraction** — the He-3/platinum-group economy already written into
`mercury.md` is a *later* upgrade (Phase 3, below), not the founding
reason. Mars's own tunnel-boring expertise, already developed and
patented building its dozen underground starter colonies, gets
contracted out through a new company, **Terebra Subsurface**, to
build the Deep Ring in a joint UN venture — the UN's own test of its
newly grown capital-organizing muscle, flush with India's post-veto-
seat funding, before trying anything bigger.

**Separately from Vishvakarma, "the old Western refinery"** — a true
deep-Belt facility, distinct from the L4 site, still under Western
control throughout — goes down again sometime in this stretch and
gets rebuilt more carefully, specifically to avoid repeating
Vishvakarma's own unsafe, rapid-scaling chaos. That leaves two major
industrial anchors running in parallel by the 2200s. Vishvakarma's own
stretches as the sole working supplier during the Western refinery's
downtime become real, citable leverage — directly feeding the already-
established fact that Bharat's UN veto seat came from "real leverage
(funding, tech dependency), not fairness"
(`earth-political-structure.md`): this is where that leverage was
actually built. Still unnamed, open for later.

**~2200–2250 — Jupiter fills up, drawing the system's more conforming
settlers** — a comply-and-prosper culture fitting the TGC/JDC
institutional machinery already written for it. As Ganymede becomes
more and more central to outer-system food production, its own water
demand eventually outstrips what's local — **Saturn's first reason
for existing is as Jupiter's water supplier**, not a project of its
own, and it lags Jupiter's own settlement accordingly.

Meanwhile on Earth: severe brain drain (the best and brightest of the
old world long since gone) plus already-declining birth rates open a
real economic vacuum, which Africa and the broader Global South —
already on a strong growth trajectory — move to fill, Earthside first.
As their rise reshapes Earth's dominant culture, old first-world
religious communities feel real, uncomfortable strain (not quite war)
— a follow-on wave of mostly old-first-world and Russian/Eastern-
European emigration, directly seeding the Slavic thread's second
source alongside Korolev and Vernadsky's own earlier technical elite.

**The UN, flush with India's money and effectively unchecked for half
a century, turns genuinely heavy-handed in this same stretch** — and
Saturn's real settlement boom, as opposed to its quiet existence as
Jupiter's water tap, starts here as a direct reaction: the solar
"highlands" become the natural draw for people who want nothing to do
with UN dominion. A platinum-group-metals "gold rush" rumor — someone
claiming to have scooped raw PGMs straight out of the rings, never
conclusively confirmed, deliberately legend-shaped rather than settled
fact — is what actually triggers the real surge of settlement rather
than the slow trickle before it.

**Saturn's resulting population is genuinely mixed, with Scottish and
Irish settlers giving the whole culture its founding vocabulary and
clan structure.** Russian and Cossack settlers converge into the same
anti-authority structure for the same underlying reason, but
consolidate rather than scatter — where the Scottish and Irish
population split across multiple allied Families under two separate
Combines (Dunmore, Ardcarrick), the Russian/Cossack contingent holds
together as one tight, cohesive bloc from early on: **the Stanitsa**,
replacing the name Clan Glenshiel previously carried in `saturn.md`
— a real historical term, the actual word for a Cossack settlement,
same naming logic as Mercat using the real word for "market" rather
than an invented one. Bible Belt American and South American
religious settlers converge into the same broad current for the same
reason — genuine, if less acute, discomfort with Earth's shifting
religious center of gravity — without needing their own separate
Combine yet.

## Phase 2: the largest wave, then the Rupture (~2250–2280)

**~2250 — the new nations are finally strong enough for their own
major push.** With most of the old world already drained of its best
and brightest, Africa and the broader Global South's own wave becomes
the single largest exodus of the entire pre-Rupture era, running
roughly 2250–2280 — the direct root of West African/Nigerian becoming,
by "now," the corpus's strongest demographic thread
(`demographics.md`). **Venus gets its own early, modest research-
platform presence somewhere in this same broad window** — real but
unglamorous, decades before anyone lavishes real money on it.

**~2280 — flows straight into the Rupture**, fully fixed already:
the Seven Day War, the Drift Years, the Massacre at Thisbe Station,
the Ceres Broadcast, the OPRA moment, the Anti-Solar Accord.

## Phase 3: scarcity (the 2330s → now, ~2350, still ongoing)

The one phase that directly continues the corpus's own demographic-
decline thesis: expansion now because the easy reserves are already
spent, not because anyone wants to keep pushing outward. Luna's own
easy reserves running thin (`flashpoints.md`'s Lunar resource
depletion entry) is the root cause threading through all of it —
Ganymede's impractical Jupiter-atmosphere research, and now Saturn's
genuinely practical atmospheric skimming operation.

**Mercury's economy gets its real upgrade here** — the He-3 and
platinum-group extraction economy `mercury.md` already describes
layers onto the original research-only founding only once Lunar
depletion makes the brutal trip newly worth making, not before.

**Saturn's atmospheric-skimming/charter fight (`saturn.md`) reaches
its current, still-unresolved state here** — raw and recent by design,
the newest frontier in the corpus, which is exactly why it's full of
live flashpoints and no named individual leadership yet rather than
anything settled.

**Venus becomes the newest and shiniest of all of them, very
recently, within the last 50 years.** Wealth generated by Saturn's own
platinum rush and by Mercury's Perihelion Traders/Sunbird trade
concentrates — as wealth always does in this corpus — into a small
number of hands, who lavish it on the Venusian Project: a quiet
research-and-modest-medical presence turned up to the full luxury-
wellness complex already written into `venus.md`. That's the real
reason Venus reads as the least lived-in, most recently maxed-out
place in the whole corpus: it genuinely is.
