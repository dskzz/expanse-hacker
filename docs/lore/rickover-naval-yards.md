# Rickover Naval Yards

Status: locked, 2026-11-24. Ninth full station write-up — the last
item from the original stations list. Earth's own shipbuilder and
weapons producer, named for Hyman Rickover, the real father of the US
nuclear navy. The political and economic character is already fully
established in `corporations.md`'s "Earth's military-industrial
decline" section; this page adds the physical place and its specific
location data, rather than repeating the corporate analysis.

**LocationChain:** `UN:LUNA:RICKOVER`
**Real orbital anchor:** Luna, plus several distinct orbital
facilities — not one building, a dispersed complex.

## Physical structure

A large, sprawling complex on Luna's surface — old enough that its
layout reads as accretion, not planning, additional halls and docks
added onto the original footprint over generations rather than built
to one coherent design. The orbital facilities are a separate,
physically distinct tier: several freestanding stations in Lunar
orbit handling final assembly and fitting-out for hulls too large to
finish surface-side, linked to the ground complex by regular
shuttle traffic rather than any single umbilical structure. Visibly
old in a way Slayton Construction Yards or Lodestar's Belt operations
aren't — a place that's been added to for far longer than it's been
rebuilt.

## What's already established elsewhere

- **The decline, not a flat replacement:** Rickover's still huge,
  still the backbone for hulls and legacy systems, still employs
  enormous numbers of people — but it's losing ground on cutting-edge
  modern armaments to Mars's Calloway and Ashdown Munitions as the
  joint UNN–MRN fleet increasingly favors better Mars-sourced designs
  (`corporations.md`).
- **A civilian division under the same roof**, the real Boeing
  pattern — leaning harder into commercial shipbuilding as a hedge
  against the declining military side, rather than a separate company
  needing to be invented.
- **The software layer tells the same story as the hardware:**
  Rickover's control software and drivers are genuinely error-prone
  and outdated, a direct symptom of Earthstock's already-established
  "over-trusting of its own PKI" weakness (`os-lineages.md` §2) —
  hardware decline and software decline as the same underlying story
  at two different layers.
- **The labor hook:** Rickover's workforce watching contracts drift
  to Mars while their own yards get starved of modern work is exactly
  the grievance the New International (`earth-political-structure.md`)
  would organize around.
