# Industrial & Shipbuilding Stations — Reference

Status: locked, 2026-11-24. Lighter-tier companion to the full
station pages — real orbital anchors and LocationChain addresses for
the shipbuilding-supply-chain and industrial-components companies
already established in `corporations.md`, each with one or two
concrete details rather than a full page. Promote any of these to a
full page if a scenario ends up needing one.

**LocationChain convention used throughout:**
`[Domain]:[Region/Body]:[Facility]` — matching the existing worked
examples in `rfc2350-addressing-grammar.md` (`MRN:ALPHAFLEET:VANGUARD`).
LocationChain reflects real physical/network routing position, not
political ownership — the same distinction already established for
Doris (`corporations.md`): a station's LocationChain has to stay
honest about where it actually, currently is.

## Anchorpoint Shipwrights

**LocationChain:** `MDR:MARS-ORBIT:ANCHORPOINT`
**Anchor:** Mars orbit.

Mars's civilian/general-purpose shipbuilding half, the direct rival
to Lodestar Shipyards without Garrison's military specialization. An
orbital facility rather than surface-based, keeping it clear of
Mariner Valley's own capability-is-authority politics on the ground.

## Garrison Shipworks

**LocationChain:** `MDR:DEIMOS:GARRISONSW`
**Anchor:** Near Deimos.

Mars's naval specialist, the shipbuilding partner to Calloway and
Ashdown Munitions. Sited near Deimos deliberately — direct proximity
to MDR's own helium-3 mining operations there (`mars-political-
structure.md`), giving naval vessels fuel-logistics access without
routing through Mars orbit or Mariner Valley at all.

## Corelli Drive Systems

**LocationChain:** `MDR:MARS-ORBIT:CORELLI`
**Anchor:** Mars orbit, near Mariner Valley.

Propulsion and engines only — supplies Lodestar, Anchorpoint, and
Garrison alike rather than competing with any of them, the GE/Rolls-
Royce-to-Boeing relationship. Centrally positioned in Mars orbit
specifically so none of its three customers has an obvious logistics
advantage over the others.

## Standard Fabrication Co.

**LocationChain:** `MDR:MARINER-VALLEY:STANDARD-FAB`
**Anchor:** Mariner Valley.

Industrial components and, by extension, robotics/heavy equipment —
assembling machines being the natural next step from making the
parts. Based directly in Mariner Valley, at the physical center of
Mars's engineering economy alongside Helion Power and Keystone
Embedded Systems.

## Patchwright Conversions

**LocationChain:** `BELT:VESTA:PATCHWRIGHT`
**Anchor:** Near 4 Vesta — a real, large, well-known main-belt
asteroid.

Refit and conversion of existing hulls, the value-chain step up from
Driftwood Salvage & Reclamation's pure scrap. Sited near Vesta for
the same reason a lot of Belt industry clusters near large bodies:
real mass and real infrastructure already there to build against.

## Skipjack Craft Co.

**LocationChain:** `BELT:CERES:SKIPJACK`
**Anchor:** Near Ceres.

Small craft and shuttles — a genuinely different market from
interplanetary vessels, the general-aviation-to-airliner distinction.
Sited near Ceres deliberately: a small-craft maker needs to be close
to where the actual population and daily short-hop traffic is, not
out in a quiet construction gap the way a big integrator would want.

## Cutwater Yards

**LocationChain:** `BELT:PALLAS:CUTWATER`
**Anchor:** Near 2 Pallas — a real main-belt asteroid.

A smaller, ordinary general-purpose builder, not a niche specialist.
Named for the cutwater, the forward edge of a ship's bow that splits
the water.

## Millrace Shipworks

**LocationChain:** `BELT:HYGIEA:MILLRACE`
**Anchor:** Near 10 Hygiea — a real main-belt asteroid.

Another small, ordinary local builder, same modest scale as Cutwater.
Named for the channel that drives a mill wheel — workaday industrial
flavor, no grander ambition implied.
