# Ship Class Codex

Status: tracking/reference doc, created 2026-11-25. Parallel to
`equipment-codex.md` — a growing reference for named ship classes
that don't belong to one location file, built once and reused
wherever a scenario needs a specific vessel type rather than a
generic "a ship."

## Ballista-class (MRN, Mars)

A dedicated precision-bombardment ship class, named for the real
ancient Roman siege weapon specifically known for being the
*accurate*, engineered alternative to cruder siege weapons of its
era — fitting both the doctrine and Mars's own established Roman-
office theming (`mariner-valley.md`'s Consuls, Senate-derived
structure) without reaching for anything new. Built at Rickover
Naval Yards (`rickover-naval-yards.md`), MDR's existing military
shipbuilding site.

**Doctrine:** guided, variable-yield munitions for proportionate,
targeted strikes — a small installation gets a small, precise hit;
a larger target gets more yield, but always aimed, never just mass
thrown and hoped for. Genuinely more usable in a limited conflict
than raw kinetic bombardment (hurling a rock, or ramming a craft at
speed), which only makes sense at the scale of total, civilization-
ending war. Mars has no institutional reason to avoid refining this
capability the way the UN does (below) — MDR is a sovereign military
power, not a fragile multilateral body whose legitimacy depends on
never looking like it's preparing to use force.

## Zephyr-class (Perihelion Traders, Earth)

A dedicated Mercury Run merchant vessel, named for the real generic
word for a light, favorable wind — fitting, now that sails are
genuinely part of the design, without leaning on mythology the way
"Icarus" or similar would. Full commercial and route detail lives in
`mercury.md`; recorded here because it's a genuinely distinct hull
type, not a flavor label on an ordinary hauler.

**What makes it a real, separate class:** a standard fusion drive for
primary propulsion, plus a deployable solar-sail array no ordinary
interplanetary ship bothers carrying, because nowhere else in the
system has enough flux to make the extra mass worth it. The sail does
two different jobs depending on direction — direct outward thrust on
the departure leg, and the real "shed angular momentum by angling
nearly edge-on" spiral-in maneuver on the inbound leg, since radiation
pressure can never push toward the Sun directly. Crewed by **the
Sunbirds**, rotating on a dose-budget-driven cycle rather than a
fixed calendar figure — roughly one round trip per tour, shorter
during solar maximum, longer during solar minimum (`mercury.md`).

**Full system-by-system detail — rigging, suits, active shielding,
the safe room's real water/consumables shielding, the Corona Watch,
and the waste-ballast mechanic — lives in `mercury.md`**, not
duplicated here; this entry stays the hull-type summary.

## Rorqual-class (the Ringers, Saturn)

The Ringers' signature ring-working hull, named for the real baleen
whale family known for lunge-feeding — break the target first, then
take in the resulting slush through a wide intake maw, rather than
grapple-and-haul a discrete chunk the way a Belt rock-hopper would.
Fits the real physical difference between the two environments: Belt
asteroids are discrete, haulable bodies; Saturn's rings are a dense
field of ice from dust to boulders, which break-and-scoop handles far
better than grappling ever could. Full operational and political
detail — clan-held claim boundaries, the plow ships that keep lanes
clear for them, and the separately-run atmospheric skimming operation
that doesn't use this hull at all — lives in `saturn.md`, not
duplicated here.

## What the UN deliberately doesn't have

Worth recording as its own entry, not just an absence: the UN has no
dedicated bombardment-ship class at all, on purpose
(`earth-political-structure.md`). Any sufficiently massive UN vessel
*could* theoretically be weaponized by aiming it at something — pure
physics, not a designed capability — but the UN has never built or
fielded anything optimized for that, because refining the capability
would mean planning to actually use it more often and more precisely,
which directly undercuts the "this is an unthinkable last resort,
never a planned one" logic its deterrent depends on. A standing UN
bombardment fleet would also be a far more alarming, undeniable
political fact than "technically physics allows it" — exactly the
kind of thing an institution whose legitimacy rests on restraint
can't afford to build.

## Open threads

- Belt smuggling/light craft, the UN's own more mundane fleet
  vessels, troop transports, and anything MDR fields beyond
  Ballista-class are all still open for whenever a scenario needs
  one.
- No named Ballista-class, Zephyr-class, or Rorqual-class vessels or

  ring-plane/E-ring lanes, and the atmospheric skimmers working
  Saturn's upper cloud layers — are functional/role categories rather
  than a single distinctive hull type each, and stay in `saturn.md`
  rather than getting their own codex entry, at least for now.
