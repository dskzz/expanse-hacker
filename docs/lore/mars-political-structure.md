# Mars Political Structure (replaces "MCRN")

Status: locked naming decision, documented retroactively 2026-11-21.
The MCRN (Martian Congressional Republic Navy) → MDR/MRN rename itself
was decided and swept across the corpus earlier in this project, but
never got its own lore doc the way the Belt's OPA→OSL/ASA split did
(`belt-political-structure.md`). This file closes that gap and adds a
genuine new piece of worldbuilding: Mars had read as too homogeneous,
the same "one org speaks for everyone" thinness the Belt had before
the OSL/ASA split and the corporate landscape had before the
Continuity Group — so Mars gets its own internal fault line too.

## Martian Democratic Republic (MDR) / Martian Republic Navy (MRN)

The nation and its navy. "Democratic Republic" is a deliberate real-
world echo — the same ironic naming convention real authoritarian-
leaning states have used (the Congo DRC, East Germany, North Korea's
own "Democratic People's Republic") — chosen because it fits Mars's
actual disciplined, militarized culture: a state that genuinely
oversells "democratic" relative to how it actually runs, the same way
those real states did. Not a 1:1 rename of MCRN; a different, real
naming logic applied to the same role.

## The Commonwealth of Elysium

Mars isn't one homogeneous culture any more than the Belt is one
faction. The **Commonwealth of Elysium** — usually just "the
Commonwealth" — is a devolved, self-governing region occupying
**Elysium** (real Martian geography: the Elysium Mons volcanic
province, roughly antipodal to Mariner Valley on the opposite side of
the planet), folded into MDR rather than conquered by it.

**Founding:** before Mars unified under MDR, Elysium was colonized
separately — a Sino-Russian/Eurasian-coalition venture racing the
mostly Western-coalition effort that became the Mariner Valley core.
When unification happened, Elysium didn't get absorbed outright: it
negotiated genuine devolved autonomy — its own regional assembly, its
own economic customs — as the price of joining rather than resisting.

**Political culture, not a regime label — and specifically not the
"Eastern collective = commie" cliché.** Collectivism as a genuine
cultural value (real, found across Confucian-influenced societies
independent of any specific political-economic system) is a different
thing entirely from 20th-century Marxist-Leninist communism, and
conflating the two is exactly the lazy move this corpus keeps working
to avoid. Elysium's capital, **Datong (大同)**, is named for the real
Confucian political-philosophy concept — "Great Unity" or "Great
Harmony," from the *Book of Rites*' "Liyun" chapter, describing an
idealized, egalitarian society sharing resources communally — a
concept that predates Marxism by over two thousand years. That's the
founding ideal the name honors.

**LocationChain:** `ELYSIUM:DATONG` — Elysium's real devolved
autonomy (its own Consular seat, its frank authoritarian-corporatist
character) earns it a top-level domain of its own rather than nesting
under MDR, the same logic as giving Ganymede's shared jurisdiction
its own identity instead of picking a side. Added as part of the
corpus-wide LocationChain retrofit pass.

**The modern reality has evolved past that ideal, openly rather than
by denial.** What started as something closer to Datong's genuine
collectivist vision has, over generations, become frank — not
hidden — **authoritarian political control plus heavily
government-regulated corporatism**, the real-world pattern sometimes
called "state capitalism" (modern China's actual hybrid of one-party
political control and substantial, state-directed market activity,
rather than classic centrally-planned Soviet-style communism; also
visible in things like Singapore's government-linked companies).
Elysium didn't abandon Datong's ideal and hide the fact — it evolved
past it and stopped bothering to perform otherwise, which makes it a
genuine contrast with MDR rather than a mirror of it: **MDR performs
a legitimacy it doesn't fully have** (real elections, "Democratic
Republic" branding, capability-is-authority hiding underneath,
`mariner-valley.md`). **Elysium has stopped performing entirely** —
openly authoritarian-corporatist, Datong's name carrying the founding
idealism the way "the United Nations" still carries 1940s idealism
Earth's actual politics mostly outgrew.

The Commonwealth's own name deliberately pulls from more than one
real precedent at once regardless of this evolution: Puerto Rico's
actual "Commonwealth" status (self-governing, folded into a larger
nation, neither fully separate nor fully absorbed), the real
Commonwealth of Independent States (fitting Elysium's own Eurasian
colonial lineage), and the British Commonwealth's "tied by shared
history, not command" looseness. None of these are borrowed wholesale;
the term just happens to be real, flexible, and already proven to
mean exactly this shape of arrangement.

**Ongoing friction:** devolution as a live, unresolved political
question rather than settled history — autonomy referendums, disputes
over how much the Commonwealth's assembly can override MDR doctrine
from Mariner Valley, resentment on both sides that never quite boils
into actual separatism. This is a standing, textured political fact
about Mars, not a subplot about rebellion.

**A seat on the Consular College, won rather than inherited.**
Elysium wasn't one of the dozen founding colonies that consolidated
into Mariner Valley (`mariner-valley.md`) — it was colonized entirely
separately. When it negotiated its devolution deal, it negotiated its
own seat on the already-existing Consular College too: a
**thirteenth seat**, added later, distinct from the original twelve.

**The one real exception to the College's otherwise-ceremonial
character.** The original twelve seats represent founding colonies
that are, per the Martian Congress's own malapportionment problem,
increasingly hollow — old, shrinking, symbolically important but
materially diminishing. Elysium's seat represents a bloc that's
currently, actually that strong: population, economy, and industrial
capability rivaling the rest of Mars **combined** — the Commonwealth
is formally just one devolved region of MDR, but by far the single
strongest independent power bloc on the planet, counterweighted only
by everyone else added together. Formal ceremonial powerlessness
doesn't survive contact with real material power at that scale:
nobody can actually afford to treat Elysium's Consul as a figurehead
the way they can the other eleven, regardless of what the College's
rules formally say — the same underlying logic as "capability is
authority" elsewhere in MDR, just surfacing inside the ceremonial
body instead of the executive branch. Elysium's seat is the live
exception that proves the rule for everyone else's.

## The Mariner Valley Caucus

Elysium has a formal identity (the Commonwealth, its 13th Consular
seat, its own bloc weight). The rest of MDR, when it needs to act as
a counterweight, now has one too: **the Mariner Valley Caucus** —
deliberately named to sound like an accident of geography, "just the
representatives from Mariner Valley," rather than a determined effort
to counter Elysium's interests specifically. Same oversold-
innocuousness naming logic as "Democratic Republic" itself and the
Sol Industrial Trust's own name (`corporations.md`) — call the real
thing something bland enough that it reads as incidental.

First real, live use: siding with the UN against Elysium-backed
Belt interests in the Thisbe helium-3 dispute (`thisbe-station.md`) —
the issue that actually activates this split rather than leaving it
as background texture.

## Fusion fuel independence: Phobos and Deimos

Mars's own surface is a poor native source of helium-3 — its thin
atmosphere and patchy residual magnetic field meaningfully reduce
solar-wind implantation compared to a truly airless body, nowhere
near as efficient an accumulator as the Moon's regolith
(`armstrong-city.md`). But Mars has an obvious local source in orbit:
**Phobos and Deimos**, both small, airless, heavily cratered moons —
physically the same environment as Luna's surface or an asteroid,
directly exposed to solar wind for billions of years, just
accumulating at a somewhat lower rate given Mars's greater distance
from the Sun. A real, workable local source, not an import
dependency — and already present in the corpus's inherited naming
conventions without previously meaning anything (the old vault
network list's `MDR-OPS-PHOBOS`/`MDR-OPS-DEIMOS`, frozen but
inspirational): these are MDR's actual helium-3 mining operations.

Thematically, not just conveniently: Mars securing its own
independent fusion-fuel supply rather than depending on Earth/Luna
for something this strategically critical is exactly the kind of
self-sufficiency a nation this insecure about its unequal
relationship with Earth would have prioritized early. Mars isn't
vulnerable to an Earth fuel chokehold the way it might otherwise be —
earned independence, not an accident.

## Still open

- Exact unification date relative to the rest of the corpus's timeline
  isn't pinned down yet — should probably predate "now" (~2350) by a
  comfortable margin, same reasoning as the Belt's fifty-year
  divergence window.
- Whether Elysium's capability-based security root (MDR's declared
  "root model" per `os-lineages.md` §2) actually differs in any
  mechanical way from Mariner Valley's, or whether the difference is
  purely political/cultural with the same underlying tech — leaning
  toward the latter unless a design reason emerges to make it
  mechanically distinct too.
