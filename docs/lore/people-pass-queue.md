# People Pass Queue

Status: tracking doc, created 2026-11-24. Per the standing agreement
to finish the big structural worldbuilding passes (corporations,
politics, stations) before turning to named individuals, this file
collects every role/figure the corpus has already implied or
explicitly flagged as needing a name later — so nothing gets lost
between now and whenever the people pass actually happens. Add to
this list as new unnamed roles come up; strike entries once named
(moving the name into its proper home file, not leaving it only
here).

    corporate to act on it.

  - The ASA-side handler/contact the asset reports to.

- **The UN's head of state analog** (`earth-political-structure.md`)
  — kept deliberately generic/institutional so far; unnamed.
- **The New International's leadership** (`earth-political-structure.md`)
  — both its open, marginalized-political-movement face and its
  clandestine resistance-network face; unnamed.
- **The Tripartite Ganymede Commission's representatives**
  (`ganymede-condominium.md`) — no named figure for any of the three
  parties' seats (MDR, UN, or the Belt's token presence).
- **The Rockline Compact** (`corporations.md`) — coordinates in self-
  defense against the Trust's suspected collusion; no named leading
  figure across its member companies (Reyes-Petrov, Lodestar, VARS,
  Longreach, Driftwood).
- **Sol Industrial Trust member executives** (`corporations.md`) —
  all four members (Okafor-Lindgren, Hauptmann & Voskuijlen, and the
  other two) have company-level entries but no named CEOs or officers

- **Haven Corrections Group** (`corporations.md`) — no named
  leadership for the Earth–Mars joint-venture private prison company.
- **Doris** (`corporations.md`) — explicitly "a thin, barely-staffed
  local board"; no administrator or figurehead named, which may
  actually suit the place (a flag-of-convenience jurisdiction
  arguably works better anonymous) but is worth a deliberate decision
  rather than a permanent default.
- **The UN Secretary-General** (`earth-political-structure.md`) — no
  name yet for the UN's own top executive.
- **The Proportionality Caucus's leadership** (`earth-political-structure.md`)
  — the loyal-opposition faction pushing to reapportion UN
  representation to match real demographic/economic weight; no named
  figures yet.
- **Bloc UN delegation heads** (`earth-political-structure.md`) — each
  of the nine blocs sends its own appointed delegation; none named yet
  for any bloc.
- **The Fleet Staff** (`earth-political-structure.md`) — the UN's
  actual operational command, four seats: a Home Fleet Admiral, a
  Frontier Fleet Admiral, a Marine Commandant, and a Merchant Marine
  liaison; none named yet.
- **The Joint Military Council** (`earth-political-structure.md`) —
  nine seats, one per bloc, deliberately multi-bloc and consensus-
  gated by design; none named yet, though each bloc's own political
  character (`earth-blocs.md`) suggests whether its seat-holder is a
  civilian or, as with the Zhongguo bloc, plausibly a retired general.
  Whoever eventually exploits (or discovers) the rogue-command
  technical vulnerability is a natural future figure.
- **Each bloc's head of government and UN delegation head**
  (`earth-blocs.md`) — nine blocs, none named: the Atlantic Zone's
  rotating-Presidency holder, the African Union's Assembly chair, the
  Zhongguo bloc's paramount leader, Bharat's Prime Minister, the
  Eurasian Union's Supreme Council chair, Patria Grande's pro-tempore
  president, the Three Seas bloc's rotating host, ASEAN and Others'
  rotating chair, and the OIC bloc's own Iran/Saudi/Pakistan-brokered
  leadership.
- **OSL's own UN ambassador** (`earth-political-structure.md`,
  `belt-political-structure.md`) — the Belt's Permanent Observer
  mission at New Delhi has no named head yet.
- **The four specialized agencies** (`earth-political-structure.md`)
  — no named head for the International Court, the UN Monetary
  Authority, the Public Health Directorate, or the Commons and
  Environment Directorate.
- **The UN Committee Against Torture** (`earth-political-structure.md`)
  — no named chair or members yet.
- **Each bloc's own domestic party leadership tied to its pet-issue
  cleavage** (`earth-blocs.md`, `earth-resources-and-pressures.md`) —
  the Atlantic Zone's globalist-vs-nativist split, Bharat's

  `mars-political-structure.md`) — none named yet.
