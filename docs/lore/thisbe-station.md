# Thisbe Station

Status: locked, 2026-11-24. Seventh full station write-up, and the
corpus's actual founding-trauma site — the Expanse-decoupled
replacement for "Anderson Station" (`os-lineages.md` §1, §7). Settled
on the deliberate "uneasy third way": neither fully redeemed nor a
dead memorial.

## The real rock, best understanding

**88 Thisbe** — a real, fairly large main-belt asteroid, roughly 200+
km across, among the couple dozen biggest bodies in the belt.
Discovered in 1866. Classified as a carbonaceous-type body (possibly
specifically B-type, the same family as Bennu, the real OSIRIS-REx
target) — confident on size and general classification, less so on
fine orbital specifics, which should be treated loosely. Carbonaceous
asteroids are exactly the type richest in volatiles: water ice,
hydrated minerals, organic compounds — the same resource category
Frostline Harvesting's whole business runs on, and the same stuff
feeding deuterium extraction for fusion fuel. Sits at an ordinary,
accessible position within the main belt, on normal shipping lanes —
not an exotic outer-system location, not a special pilgrimage site.
People pass through constantly without meaning to.

## The massacre and the reform window

~2290: a surrender message gets flagged low-priority, drifts through
Earth's civilian filters, and is silently dropped. Elias Voss orders
an assault based on silence the network itself manufactured — the
founding trauma of this entire setting. The administrative authority
running Thisbe at the time was Earth/UN-adjacent, consistent with the
message being filtered by "Earth's civilian filters" in the first
place.

The **Ceres Broadcast** (~2290s–2299) made the massacre public
knowledge system-wide, and the old authority's credibility didn't
survive the scandal. In the resulting vacuum — nobody from the UN,
Mars, or a Belt org stepped in to replace it — Thisbe's surviving
workforce organized into a genuine **workers' cooperative**, self-
governed rather than imposed. This is plausibly the earliest real-
world proof of concept behind **the New International**
(`earth-political-structure.md`) — not pure theory, actual history.

## Strangled from outside, not corrupted from within

Deliberately not an Animal Farm story — the co-op didn't betray its
own ideals from the inside. It worked, genuinely, for a real stretch.
What actually wore it down was the same mechanism running elsewhere
in this corpus: inner-system finance capital (Hauptmann & Voskuijlen-
style debt leverage, market undercutting from Trust-adjacent volatile/
ice interests) picking at a resource-rich but politically inconvenient
cooperative until it couldn't compete on its own terms. Structural
defeat, not moral failure — capital quietly winning because the rules
favor capital, the same pattern running through Ceres's MFN framework
and the Rockline Compact's whole grievance.

## Space Cuba: the actual present-day shape

Still a cooperative, in name and mostly in practice — hollowed out,
not thriving, not dead. The real analogy worth using directly: small,
ideologically awkward, survives despite a much larger power's
hostility because crushing it outright costs more than tolerating it
does. The UN can't move against Thisbe without reopening the
massacre's own wound publicly — the same broadcast that created the
reform window in the first place would make any crackdown now a
system-wide scandal. Thisbe can't fully break free either — it still
needs inner-system markets and capital it has no real way to replace.
Neither side can afford to act, so neither does, and the stalemate
just continues.

**Chronic underemployment, and — the part that actually matters for
play — chronically underpatched software.** Nobody with real capital
wants to invest in a place with this history and this politics, so
the cooperative is permanently cash-strapped. A station that can't
afford proper IT maintenance is a sympathetic, believable reason for
genuinely vulnerable network infrastructure — the same shape as
Shackleton's neglect (`armstrong-city.md`), different root cause:
underinvestment born of squeeze, not abandonment born of irrelevance.

## The helium-3 cluster: a live complication, not settled history

Recently discovered: an unusually rich, localized pocket of helium-3-
bearing regolith — plausibly exposed by some specific impact event
that uncovered ancient, long-undisturbed surface material, rather
than a uniform planet-wide deposit. Solar-wind-implanted helium-3
isn't exclusively lunar; it accumulates on any long-exposed airless
surface, just at a lower rate farther from the Sun — a genuinely
unusual, surprising find for a Belt body, not a rule-break.

This threatens the equilibrium above right when the squeeze was
working: the UN wanted Thisbe weak and dependent, and a real windfall
undermines that. **Genuinely open, not resolved:** does external
pressure to seize or control Thisbe intensify now that the stakes are
higher, or does the temptation of real wealth to manage pull at the
cooperative's own leadership — the exact corruption it was built to
avoid, approached from a different direction than Animal Farm's
internal betrayal, but a live risk all the same?
