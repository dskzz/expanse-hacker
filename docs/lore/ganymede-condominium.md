# The Ganymede Condominium

Status: locked, 2026-11-24. First full write-up in the stations pass
following the corporate-landscape work (`corporations.md`). Fixes a
gap the same way the Belt's OSL/ASA split and Mars's Commonwealth of
Elysium did: Ganymede isn't a monolith, and no single power ever
managed to claim it outright.

**LocationChain:** `TGC:GANYMEDE` — no single faction owns this
place, so the chain anchors on the Tripartite Ganymede Commission
itself rather than picking a side, added as part of the corpus-wide
LocationChain retrofit pass.

**Condominium** is used here as the actual real term of art in
international law — two or more sovereign powers jointly governing
one territory by splitting *function*, not geography. Real precedent:
Anglo-Egyptian Sudan, the New Hebrides under British-French
condominium rule; closer in spirit, Antarctica's treaty system and
Svalbard's multinational research-access regime. Ganymede became
valuable enough — agriculturally, strategically — that no single
power could take it, and everyone settled for a negotiated slice of
function instead of a flag.

## The three-way division

Lines are fluid, not absolute silos — general tendencies the
Condominium breaks down over, not hard walls. Nobody's presence
anywhere is strictly zero.

**MDR — orbital infrastructure.** Mirrors, lenses, sensors: the
civilian/economic orbital hardware that supplements light and heat to
the dome clusters. Fits Mars's engineering-disciplined culture
directly. This is real economic leverage, not incidental — MDR
controlling the orbital mirrors means MDR holds a literal chokepoint
over the colony's food supply, the same shape of existential leverage
Meridiani Systems already holds over Mars's own atmosphere
(`corporations.md`). Deliberately kept sharp, not softened.

**MDR + UN jointly — orbital defense.** Not Mars alone, and not a new
mechanism invented for Ganymede: the same **UNN–MRN joint fleet**
already established for Sol's main battle fleet, extended to local
defense here. No power sharing a strategically valuable location with
a rival would ever accept the other holding sole military control —
splitting defense from economic infrastructure is what keeps this an
actual negotiated condominium instead of Mars quietly running the
place.

**UN/Earth — research and its IP.** The universities, and almost all
of the resulting seed stock, growth-medium chemistry, and patents —
arising naturally from Earth owning the actual institutions, not from
any formal land grant. This is very likely the same institutional
lineage behind **Greenhollow Agricultural Systems'** proprietary
licensing racket (`corporations.md`) — not two unrelated facts.
Mars keeps most of its own scientists local, concentrated on its own
terraforming work, so its research footprint here is real but
deliberately small — until the bounty gets big enough to be worth
muscling in for anyway (see Tensions, below).

**The same universities' long game: Jupiter's atmosphere itself.**
Gas giants hold a far larger helium-3 reservoir than any solid body
ever could — real planetary science, not invented — but Jupiter's
gravity well is the deepest in the solar system after the Sun's own,
and the fuel cost of launching anything, let alone harvested cargo,
back out of it currently dwarfs whatever it would be worth. That
math is exactly why this stays a funded, long-term research line
rather than an active operation: early-stage aerostat and probe
studies into the upper atmosphere's survivable pressure bands,
published papers, no extraction hardware yet. The money behind it
isn't blind optimism — Luna's own helium-3 reserves are plausibly
running thin (`flashpoints.md`'s Lunar resource depletion entry), and
an institution that can see that crisis coming has real reason to
keep funding the one reservoir in the system big enough to actually
end it, even decades before the energy economics might ever close
the gap. The same radiation-hardening expertise already going into
the ring-moon research platforms (`jovian-irregular-moons.md`) is a
natural, direct precursor to anything that eventually has to survive
inside Jupiter's own atmosphere.

**The Belt — the actual operation.** Greenhollow and Frostline's
ground-level labor and agricultural output, run day-to-day by Belters
regardless of whose paper sits upstream. No formal seat on the
Commission proportional to this — see the backdoor, below.

## The Tripartite Ganymede Commission (TGC)

The standing coordinating body, where MDR and UN representatives (and
a real but limited Belt liaison presence) hash out joint decisions.
"Tripartite" oversells equal three-way partnership the same way
"Democratic Republic" and "Public Access" oversell their own
legitimacy elsewhere in this corpus — the Belt's seat is real, not
fictional, just not remotely equal to the other two.

**Threshold Arbitration** (`corporations.md`) almost certainly carries
a disproportionate share of its actual caseload from Commission
disputes — a condominium with this much built-in friction generates
real, constant work for a cross-domain arbiter, not abstract flavor
text.

## Tensions

- **The Belt wants real input on defense decisions**, not just ground-
  level labor with no vote on the systems sitting over its own
  workforce and infrastructure. Formally, this goes nowhere through
  the Commission's token Belt seat.
- **Earth and Mars jockey against each other** for greater relative
  influence over the joint defense arrangement — rivals forced to
  share something neither fully trusts the other with.
- **Mars muscles into research** despite its stated preference for
  keeping scientists local — the agricultural science here is
  genuinely valuable, licensable IP, not subsistence farming, and the
  bounty is big enough to be worth the reluctant effort.
- **The Belt's real leverage is technical, not political — and it's
  informal, possibly unknown to the other two parties.** Belter
  technicians physically maintain the ground-level power, comms, and
  life-support infrastructure the orbital defense network has to
  interface with somewhere. Some of them — plausibly Rockline
  Compact-adjacent — may have developed unofficial access or insight
  into the defense network simply by being the people who keep
  everything underneath it running. Not a vote. A key. Whether to
  reveal it, hold it as leverage, or use it quietly is a live fault
  line, not a settled fact.

**The general pattern worth naming:** this is the same shape as
Bedrock and Longview's exploitable friction, and Earth and Mars's
rivalry everywhere else in this corpus — wherever two or more powers
share something uneasily, there's a seam a clever operator can work.
Ganymede is just the physical, ground-level version of a pattern this
setting already runs on everywhere else.
